Each of the four field missions and SAR is worth 100 points, making 500 in total. The software does not claim a predicted competition score.

Astrobiology Q9–10 requires genuine life-detection capability, not habitability measurements alone, and discusses identification of the sampled location/depth. Equipment Q2 prohibits touching the keyboard in manual mode; Q3 specifies no auto-repeat while a key is held. These constraints need dedicated science and manipulation work, not extra navigation waypoints.

## Route-practice rule checks

- The 40-minute budget uses simulation time, while the command watchdog remains on a steady wall clock.
- Both route locations use actual DEM relief, while the selected target coordinates are ours for practice. A real 2027 target list has not been provided by organizers.
- Three-sided 4x4_50 posts are visual fixtures; seeing them in Gazebo does not mean the rover detects them.
- A stop within 1m plus a flashing-green state is evaluated at each route target. The executive pauses between targets to indicate arrival.
- The far target's DEM line of sight is obstructed. A heartbeat outage model exercises the autonomy/link-loss interface without pretending to simulate an entire radio.
- No drone is spawned; a drone landing interlock and reconnaissance workflow remain future work.
- Runtime programming, intervention penalties, physical intervention rules and breadcrumb retreat are not fully enforced. The current cancel service is a safety stop, not a legal penalty-free competition recovery.
